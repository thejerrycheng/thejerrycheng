### 👋 Hey there

I'm a **Robotics Ph.D. candidate** at the **University of Toronto**, in the [STARS Lab](https://starslab.ca/) with Prof. Jonathan Kelly. I started my Ph.D. at **NYU** in Prof. Ludovic Righetti's Machines in Motion lab.

My background runs from **multi-sensor calibration and state estimation** (radar, camera, hand-eye) to **haptics for human-robot interaction** and **visuomotor imitation learning** on robots I designed myself. These days I'm working on **mobile bimanual robots**, **dexterous manipulation**, **RL** and **world models**, aiming at a capable personal robot in **everyone's home**. I like owning the whole stack: CAD, actuators, electronics, teleop, sim, and the policies on top.
